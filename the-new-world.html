the story

somewhere between music and stillness, between the jungle and the sea, there is a place where three days feel like a whole season. the new world is not a festival. it is not a retreat. it is 100 people, one villa, and the quiet agreement to arrive fully — to move, to create, to listen, and to let the music hold you through the night.

mornings begin slowly: breath, body, and the sound of the garden waking. afternoons unfold across the pool, the workshop table, the long lunch under trees. and when the light drops gold, the music begins — and doesn't stop until the stars say so. three days. one hundred people. one small corner of bali that feels, briefly, like a new world.

the spaces

villa sasmaya

four distinct worlds within one private estate

the pool

the pool

The social heart of daylight hours. Swim, float, sprawl on a sun lounger, or find a corner of quiet. Come evening, the pool becomes a stage — soft lighting, ambient music, the water holding the mood.

  • daytime swim & rest
  • sunset sessions
  • late-night ambient sets
the garden

the garden

Frangipani and fig, dense canopy and morning light. The garden hosts our most grounded moments — movement practices, communal meals stretched across a long table, and those in-between conversations that matter most.

  • sunrise yoga & breathwork
  • long-table communal lunches
  • informal gathering
the lounge

the lounge

Inside but open to the outside — ceiling fans, low light, scattered cushions. The workshops live here. The quieter creative sessions. The conversations that need a table and a few hours.

  • creative workshops
  • somatic & sound sessions
  • evening gathering space
the rooftop

the rooftop

Open sky. The place where music lives after dark. A dancefloor that feels more like a living room — intimate enough to really hear the music, open enough to look up at the stars between tracks.

  • DJ sets

faq

questions & answers

All tickets include access to the full daytime program on your day(s) — wellness sessions, creative workshops, pool time — plus communal meals (breakfast and lunch) and the evening DJ set(s). Non-alcoholic drinks are included; alcohol is available to purchase on site. The Inner Circle tier additionally includes a shared villa room for two nights.

Only for Inner Circle ticket holders (shared villa room, Friday and Saturday nights). All other guests arrange their own accommodation nearby. Once your ticket is confirmed, we'll send you a curated list of recommended guesthouses, villas, and hotels within easy reach of Villa Sasmaya.

Fly into Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS) in Denpasar, Bali. From the airport, Gojek and Grab (ride-share apps) are affordable and reliable. The exact villa address is shared with confirmed ticket holders only — we keep it private to preserve the intimacy of the space. Travel time from the airport: plan 30–90 minutes.

Yes — the Day Pass is designed exactly for this. You choose your preferred day (Friday, Saturday, or Sunday), get full access to that day's programming and the evening set. Saturday is typically the richest day in terms of programming, if you can only choose one.

Tickets are refundable at 50% up until 30 days before the event (before 7 October 2026). Within 30 days of the event, tickets are non-refundable. You may transfer your ticket to another person at any time before the event — contact us to arrange the transfer.

the new world is a 21+ event. Valid photo ID will be required at entry. We aren't able to make exceptions to this policy.

Pack light and breathable — Bali in November is warm and occasionally rainy. A sarong or light wrap, swimwear, sunscreen, comfortable shoes (or none — this is Bali). No strict dress code; wear what feels like you. If you like to journal, bring one. Everything else, leave behind.

The event menu is largely plant-based, single-use plastics are banned on site, and we work with local Balinese vendors wherever possible. A portion of every ticket sale goes to a local environmental initiative in Bali. We encourage guests to offset their flights — resources shared with your confirmation.
